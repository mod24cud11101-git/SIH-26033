## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
### Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

We propose **FarmBridge – An AI-Powered Direct Agricultural Marketplace**, a digital platform designed to connect farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers and bulk buyers through a transparent online marketplace.

The platform enables farmers to list their agricultural products with quantity, quality, location, harvest date and expected price. Buyers can browse available products, compare prices and place individual or bulk orders without depending on multiple intermediaries.

FarmBridge also includes a smart logistics coordination system that connects available transport providers with farmers and buyers. An AI-based demand forecasting module predicts the demand for agricultural products using historical sales, seasonal trends and market information. A route optimization module helps identify efficient delivery routes based on distance, vehicle capacity and delivery locations.

The platform aims to create a fair and transparent agricultural trading ecosystem that benefits both farmers and consumers.

### How it addresses the problem

1. **Direct Market Access:** Connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers, reducing unnecessary intermediaries.
2. **Transparent Pricing:** Displays product prices, available quantities and buyer offers to support fair transactions.
3. **Bulk Order Management:** Combines orders from multiple buyers or farmer groups to improve transportation efficiency.
4. **Smart Logistics:** Matches orders with suitable transport providers and coordinates product delivery.
5. **AI Demand Forecasting:** Predicts upcoming demand and helps farmers make better selling and harvesting decisions.
6. **Route Optimization:** Suggests efficient delivery routes to reduce transportation time and cost.

### Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

- **Smart Farmer-Buyer Matching:** Recommends suitable buyers based on product type, quantity, location and price expectations.
- **Community-Based Order Pooling:** Groups nearby orders to reduce delivery costs and improve vehicle utilization.
- **Demand-Based Selling Assistant:** Provides farmers with demand predictions and suggested selling opportunities.
- **Transparent Transaction Tracking:** Maintains digital records of orders, prices, payments and deliveries.
- **Multilingual Interface:** Supports regional languages to make the platform accessible to rural users.
- **Small Farmer Support:** Allows farmers to combine their produce through FPOs to meet bulk buyer requirements.

The combination of direct trading, community-based order pooling, AI-assisted decisions and coordinated logistics makes FarmBridge a practical approach to improving agricultural market access.

## Technical Approach
### Technologies to be used

**Frontend:**
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- React.js for an interactive user interface
- Bootstrap for responsive design

**Backend:**
- Python
- Django / Django REST Framework

**Database:**

**Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning:**

**Development Tools:**
- Visual Studio Code
- Git and GitHub
- Figma for UI design

### Methodology and process for implementation

**Step 1 – User Registration:** Farmers, FPOs, buyers and transport providers register and create their profiles.

**Step 2 – Product Listing:** Farmers upload product details, quantity, quality, location and expected price.

**Step 3 – Buyer Matching:** The system identifies suitable buyers based on product requirements and location.

**Step 4 – Order Placement:** Buyers compare prices, select products and place orders.

**Step 5 – Logistics Coordination:** The system matches orders with available transport providers and suggests efficient delivery routes.

**Step 6 – AI Demand Forecasting:** Historical sales and seasonal data are analysed to predict future product demand.

**Step 7 – Order Completion:** Buyers receive their products, and transaction and delivery records are updated.

## Feasibility and Viability
### Analysis of the feasibility of the idea

FarmBridge is technically feasible because it can be developed using commonly available web technologies, open-source frameworks and machine learning libraries.

The project can initially be implemented as a web-based prototype with sample agricultural data. It can later be expanded into a mobile-friendly platform and tested with a small group of farmers, FPOs and buyers.

The modular design allows individual features such as product management, demand prediction and logistics coordination to be developed and tested independently.

### Potential challenges and risks

1. Limited internet access and digital literacy among some farmers.
2. Difficulty in verifying product quality and seller information.
3. Transportation availability and fluctuating delivery costs.
4. Insufficient historical data for accurate AI predictions.
5. Trust and payment-related concerns between buyers and sellers.
6. Maintaining accurate product availability and price information.

### Strategies for overcoming these challenges

1. Provide a simple multilingual interface with clear navigation.
2. Introduce verified farmer and FPO registration.
3. Partner with local transport providers and agricultural organizations.
4. Begin AI forecasting with available sample data and gradually improve the model using real transaction data.
5. Maintain transparent order records and integrate secure payment services through authorized providers.
6. Allow farmers to update product quantities and availability regularly.

### Potential impact on the target audience

The main beneficiaries of FarmBridge are small and marginal farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations, individual consumers, retailers, wholesalers and bulk buyers.

The platform aims to improve farmers' access to markets, reduce unnecessary supply chain costs and make agricultural products more accessible to consumers.
